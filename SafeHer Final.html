Core Features

Built for Every Scenario,
Every Hour of the Day

SafeHer covers the full spectrum of safety needs — from routine late-night commutes to genuine life-threatening emergencies.

One-Tap SOS Emergency Alert
Hold the SOS button for 2 seconds to instantly broadcast your live GPS location to all emergency contacts, nearby SafeHer users, and dial 112 automatically — even if your phone is locked.
Live Location Sharing (Always-On)
Share your real-time location with selected family, friends, or anyone — on your terms. A shareable link works even for contacts who don't have the app installed.
AI-Powered Safety Heatmap
Machine learning models process crime data, user-reported incidents, time of day, and lighting conditions to color-code every city area as Safe, Caution, or Danger — updated every 15 minutes.
Safest Route Navigation
Instead of the fastest route, SafeHer recommends the safest route — avoiding high-risk zones, poorly lit streets, and isolated areas, especially between 9 PM and 5 AM.
Fake Call & Audio Disguise
Trigger a realistic fake incoming call from a saved contact to escape uncomfortable situations. Includes timed delay and call-back scheduling.
Silent Audio + Video Recording
Activate covert background recording with one tap. Files auto-upload to encrypted cloud storage and are shared with emergency contacts — creating evidence even if the device is seized.
Community Incident Reporting
Users can anonymously pin and describe unsafe incidents. Reports are verified and fed into the ML heatmap model to warn others in real time.
Check-In & Journey Tracker
Set a journey timer. If you don't check in on arrival, SafeHer automatically alerts your contacts, sends your last known location, and escalates to 112 if no response is received.
Offline SOS via SMS Fallback
When internet is unavailable, SafeHer switches to SMS-based location broadcasting. Even in areas with zero data coverage, a basic cellular signal can trigger emergency alerts.

System Architecture

How SafeHer Works

From raw data to real-time alerts — a seamless ML pipeline designed for speed and reliability.

1
Data Ingestion
Crime records (NCRB, police APIs), user incident reports, street lighting data, and time-of-day patterns are collected and cleaned with Python + Pandas.
2
ML Risk Scoring
Scikit-learn classification models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest) predict risk scores per grid cell, trained on historical NCRB data with 87% accuracy.
3
Heatmap Generation
Risk scores are spatially aggregated using geographic clustering, rendered as color-coded heatmap tiles via Folium, stored in PostgreSQL with PostGIS extension.
4
Real-Time Alerts
User proximity to danger zones triggers proactive push alerts. SOS events propagate simultaneously to contacts, nearby users, and emergency services via multi-channel dispatch.
